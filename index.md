## About Me

👏Thank You for visiting my website👏 My name is **Sangwoo Park**, and I am currently a Master's Student at **KAIST(Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) MLAI Lab**, under the supervision of professor Sung Ju Hwang. I received my Bachelor's degree at Yonsei University, majoring in Computer Science. My research interests span over diverse topics, but mainly in Large Language Models, RAG(Retrieval and both Generation). I am especially interested in Research Automation and Scientific Discovery using agentic capabilities of LLMs, along with solving general, sophisticated multi-turn real world decision making problems of digital computer-use agents.
